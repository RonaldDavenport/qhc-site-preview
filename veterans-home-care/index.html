How VA benefits can help pay for care

Many veterans and surviving spouses may be eligible for programs that help cover the cost of care at home.

Veterans

Aid and Attendance

An additional VA benefit that may be available on top of a veteran’s basic pension. The veteran must first meet the requirements for a VA pension.

It may help offset the cost of:

  • In-home care
  • Assisted living
  • Nursing home care
Eligibility

Who may qualify

Eligibility for VA financial assistance generally depends on factors such as:

  • Active duty service, often at least 90 days
  • Service during a wartime period
  • Discharge other than dishonorable
  • Income and net worth limits
Surviving spouses

Survivors Pension

A tax-free benefit for eligible low-income surviving spouses of deceased wartime veterans. It generally requires:

  • The veteran served at least 90 days of active service
  • Discharge other than dishonorable
  • The spouse has not remarried, in most cases
  • Income and net worth within VA limits

What we do, and what the VA does

We do

  • Provide the home care these benefits can help pay for
  • Share clear information about the programs
  • Help you understand what may be available

The VA does

  • Decide who is eligible
  • Approve and pay benefits
  • Set income and service requirements

We do not provide financial assistance or determine eligibility.

Veterans home care: common questions

Still unsure? Call 832-367-9087 and ask Tieasha directly.

Does the VA help pay for home care?

The VA has programs, such as Aid and Attendance, that can help eligible veterans and surviving spouses pay for care at home. The VA decides who is eligible.

What is VA Aid and Attendance?

Aid and Attendance is an additional VA benefit that may be available on top of a veteran’s basic pension. To qualify, the veteran must first meet the requirements for a VA pension. It may help offset the cost of in-home care, assisted living, or nursing home care.

Can a surviving spouse get help paying for care?

Surviving spouses may be eligible for VA benefits. One of the most common is the Survivors Pension, a tax-free benefit for eligible low-income surviving spouses of deceased wartime veterans who have not remarried.

Do you decide whether I qualify for VA benefits?

No. The VA decides eligibility. We do not determine eligibility or provide financial assistance. We share information about these programs and provide the home care they can help pay for.
