Medicare GUIDE Program: In-Home Dementia Care

If your loved one is living with dementia or Alzheimer’s and has traditional Medicare Part A and Part B, the Medicare GUIDE program may cover up to 70 hours of in-home care.

Good to know

Many families caring for someone with dementia do not know that Medicare can pay for a caregiver to come to the home and give them a break.

Who the program is for

Medicare decides who qualifies. In general, the GUIDE program is for people who:

  1. 1

    Are living with dementia

    A diagnosis of dementia, including Alzheimer’s disease.

  2. 2

    Have traditional Medicare

    Medicare Part A and Part B, rather than a Medicare Advantage plan.

  3. 3

    Live at home

    Care comes to your loved one at home, with family helping day to day.

What the 70 hours can look like

Non-medical care at home from a trained caregiver, so the person who usually provides care can take a break.

  • Companionship and conversation
  • Supervision for safety
  • Help with bathing and dressing
  • Meal preparation
  • Keeping daily routines steady
  • Time for the family caregiver to rest

Medicare GUIDE program: common questions

Still unsure? Call 832-367-9087 and ask Tieasha directly.

What is the Medicare GUIDE program?

GUIDE is a Medicare program for people living with dementia, including Alzheimer’s disease, and the family members who care for them. One of its benefits is respite: in-home care that gives the family caregiver a break.

How many hours of in-home care does it cover?

Up to 70 hours of in-home care for people who qualify. Medicare sets the limits, so we help you check what applies to your loved one before care starts.

Who qualifies?

The program is for people living with dementia who have traditional Medicare Part A and Part B. Medicare decides eligibility. Call us and we will help you find out whether your loved one qualifies.

Does it work with a Medicare Advantage plan?

The GUIDE program is for people with traditional Medicare Part A and Part B. If your loved one has a Medicare Advantage plan, call us and we will talk through other ways families pay for care.

What does the care at home look like?

A trained caregiver comes to the home so the family caregiver can rest, work, or handle other things. Care can include companionship and supervision, help with bathing and dressing, meals, and keeping daily routines steady.

Where do you provide care?

We are based in Arcola, Texas, and serve the Houston area, including Houston, Sugar Land, Missouri City, Pearland, Katy, Conroe, Huntsville.
