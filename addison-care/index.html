Supporting Everyday Caregiving Challenges

Patients may be at risk when caregivers are not around—continuous support is essential

Daily routines can be difficult to manage without reminders

Changes in health may go unnoticed between caregiver visits

Patients may feel isolated without consistent interaction

How Addison Care Supports Patients and Caregivers

Addison Care works alongside our caregivers, providing an added layer of support that ensures patients receive continuous attention—even outside scheduled visits.

Quick alerts to caregivers and families during urgent situations

Helps patients stay consistent with prescribed routines

Tracks patient condition and provides timely updates

Encourages engagement and reduces feelings of isolation

Helps detect risks and promote safer movement

Supports early detection and better health decisions

Addison is a smart virtual care assistant designed to support both patients and caregivers through interactive guidance, health tracking, and real-time assistance.

While our caregivers provide personal, hands-on care, Addison helps ensure that support continues anytime, anywhere.

With its simple and user-friendly interface, patients can:

  • Stay on track with medications
  • Monitor their health
  • Receive reminders and alerts
  • Stay connected with caregivers and family
