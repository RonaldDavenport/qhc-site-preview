Home care in Sugar Land: common questions

Still unsure? Call 832-367-9087 and ask Tieasha directly.

Do you provide home care in Sugar Land?

Yes. Our office is in Arcola, and we serve Sugar Land, including First Colony, New Territory, Telfair, and Greatwood.

What home care services are available in Sugar Land?

Personal care, companion care, respite care, light housekeeping, specialized care, and more. All of it is non-medical care at home.

How do families pay for home care?

Families pay privately, through long-term care insurance, or with help from veterans benefits such as VA Aid and Attendance when they qualify. People living with dementia who have traditional Medicare Part A and Part B may also get up to 70 hours of in-home care through the Medicare GUIDE program. Tieasha will help you understand what applies to you.

Where do you provide care?

We are based in Arcola, Texas, and serve the Houston area, including Houston, Sugar Land, Missouri City, Pearland, Katy, Conroe, Huntsville.

How do I get started?

Request a first care conversation or call 832-367-9087. Tieasha, our founder, follows up personally within 24 hours. The first conversation does not book an appointment or start services.
