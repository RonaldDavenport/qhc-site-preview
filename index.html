Questions families ask first

Still unsure? Call 832-367-9087 and ask Tieasha directly.

What is non-medical home care?

Non-medical home care is help with everyday life at home: bathing and dressing, meals, light housekeeping, company, and relief for family caregivers. It is not nursing or clinical care.

Where does 21st Century Quality Home Care provide care?

We are based in Arcola, Texas, and serve the Houston area, including Houston, Sugar Land, Missouri City, Pearland, Katy, Conroe, Huntsville, across Harris, Fort Bend, Brazoria, Galveston, Montgomery, Waller, Walker counties.

Does the VA help pay for home care?

The VA has programs, such as Aid and Attendance, that can help eligible veterans and surviving spouses pay for care at home. The VA decides who is eligible.

We do not determine eligibility, but Tieasha can explain how families use these benefits for the kind of care we provide.

Does long-term care insurance cover home care?

Many long-term care policies cover non-medical home care. We work with families who hold policies from Genworth, John Hancock, MassMutual, Mutual of Omaha, New York Life, Northwestern Mutual, and others, and we help you check what your policy covers before care starts.

Does Medicare pay for home care?

Medicare generally does not pay for ongoing non-medical home care such as help with bathing, meals, or companionship. Families usually pay privately, through long-term care insurance, or with VA benefits.
