Long-Term Care Insurance and Home Care in Texas

If you or a loved one has a long-term care policy, it may already cover care at home. We help you check before care starts.

Good to know

Many families in Texas do not realize their long-term care policy can help pay for personal assistance at home. The coverage may already be there.

When a policy usually covers home care

In many cases, long-term care insurance covers personal assistance services at home. You may qualify if:

  1. 1

    You need help with daily living

    Help with at least two activities of daily living, such as bathing or dressing.

  2. 2

    A doctor certifies it

    A doctor certifies your condition or recommends care.

  3. 3

    Your policy is active

    Your policy benefits are in force.

What the care looks like

Non-medical personal assistance at home, so your loved one can stay in familiar surroundings.

  • Bathing, dressing, and grooming
  • Mobility support
  • Assistance with toileting
  • Meal preparation
  • Light housekeeping
  • Companionship
  • Supervision for safety
  • Respite for family caregivers

Insurers we work with

Select your insurer to read more. Your insurer decides what is covered; we help you understand your benefits.

Genworth

Caring for a loved one can be physically exhausting, emotionally heavy, and financially stressful. But if you have a Genworth long-term care policy, you may have benefits available that can help support care needs.

Genworth’s long-term care resources state that policyholders may access support for long-term care needs, and Genworth’s CareScout resources specifically reference access to home care agencies for policyholders. (genworth.com)

John Hancock

If you have a John Hancock long-term care policy, do not overlook the support that may already be available to you.

John Hancock maintains long-term care customer resources, including claims, account access, and policyholder support for individual and employer-sponsored LTC plans.

MassMutual

If you have MassMutual long-term care coverage, your policy may be part of the solution during this season of need.

MassMutual explains that long-term care insurance can help protect assets from being spent on care, and its resources describe long-term care as assistance with personal care needs and activities of daily living. MassMutual also offers hybrid LTC solutions that combine life insurance and long-term care protection.

Mutual of Omaha

Mutual of Omaha offers long-term care insurance that can cover PAS home care services for eligible clients in Texas. Many seniors and families rely on these benefits to ensure loved ones receive quality care in the comfort of their own home.

These policies are designed to give policyholders flexibility and peace of mind knowing that support is available when needed.

New York Life

If you have a New York Life long-term care policy, or long-term care options connected through New York Life resources, now is the time to review what help may be available.

New York Life’s LTC resources focus on planning for long-term care expenses, including the reality that many people eventually need care and that long-term care may involve more than facility care alone. Its AARP long-term care options page also presents guidance for people preparing for care needs.

Northwestern Mutual

Long-term care needs can arise because of aging, chronic illness, injury, or reduced ability to perform daily activities. Northwestern Mutual’s long-term care planning materials explain that people may need care in the home, in assisted living, adult day care, nursing homes, or hospice settings, and that planning helps families afford the care they need.

So if you or your loved one has Northwestern Mutual long-term care coverage, please hear this clearly: you do not have to do this alone.

Physicians Mutual

If you or your loved one has coverage connected to Physician’s Mutual, it is worth taking a closer look at your policy documents and claims resources.

Physicians Mutual’s official customer forms include references to Long-Term Care Assignment Benefits, Submit Long Term Care Claim, and Long-Term Care and Home Health Care, and its privacy materials also reference long-term care, home health care, and nursing home care coverages.

LifeSecure

If your loved one has a LifeSecure long-term care policy, there may be help available when care needs begin to grow.

LifeSecure’s official resources include long-term care insurance information, agent resources, and claims support for LTC policyholders.

Bankers Life

You May Already Have Help at Home Through Your Bankers Life Long-Term Care Policy

If you or a loved one has a Bankers Life and Casualty Company long-term care (LTC) policy, this is your reminder: you don’t have to manage caregiving alone. Many families invest in long-term care coverage for years but forget that their policy could provide help when daily care becomes challenging.

Thrivent

Thrivent offers long-term care insurance that can include PAS home care coverage in Texas. Thrivent focuses on providing flexible solutions that fit the unique needs of seniors and retirees.

These policies aim to give families more control and flexibility over care options.

Transamerica

Many families pay premiums faithfully for years but hesitate to use their long-term care benefits when the need finally comes. If you have a Transamerica long-term care policy, this is your reminder that support may be available.

Transamerica maintains long-term care policyholder resources, including LTC-related portal access and long-term care planning content.

Lincoln MoneyGuard

If you have Lincoln MoneyGuard, your policy may include long-term care benefits connected to a life insurance product. Lincoln Financial describes MoneyGuard solutions as life insurance policies with long-term care riders designed to help reimburse qualified long-term care expenses.

That means your family may have access to support when care needs become too much to manage alone.

Nationwide CareMatters

Nationwide CareMatters is a life insurance-based policy that may include long-term care benefits designed to help cover the cost of care when daily needs increase.

Many policyholders are not aware that their plan may already provide support for non-medical home care services, including Personal Assistance Services (PAS).
