Who it helps

Private duty home care is for people who find daily tasks harder than they used to, but do not need medical or nursing services. Many of the families we serve this way are veteran households.

What’s included

  • Meal preparation and cooking

Private Duty Home Care: common questions

Still unsure? Call 832-367-9087 and ask Tieasha directly.

What is private duty home care?

Non-skilled help with daily living, such as meals, housekeeping, bathing and grooming, mobility, errands, and companionship. It is for people who need support but not medical or nursing services.

How do families pay for home care?

Families pay privately, through long-term care insurance, or with help from veterans benefits such as VA Aid and Attendance when they qualify. Tieasha will help you understand what applies to you.

Where do you provide care?

We are based in Arcola, Texas, and serve the Houston area, including Houston, Sugar Land, Missouri City, Pearland, Katy, Conroe, Huntsville.

How do I get started?

Request a first care conversation or call 832-367-9087. Tieasha, our founder, follows up personally within 24 hours. The first conversation does not book an appointment or start services.
