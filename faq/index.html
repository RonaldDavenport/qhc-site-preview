Services

What services do you offer?

We provide a range of home care services, including personal care, companion care, light housekeeping, respite care, and specialized care for Alzheimer’s, dementia, ALS, and MS. Our services are tailored to meet your loved one’s unique needs with compassion and professionalism.

How do I know if home care is the right option?

If your loved one needs assistance with daily activities, companionship, or specialized care, home care can provide the support they need while allowing them to remain in the comfort of their own home.

How does Alzheimer's or dementia care differ from standard home care?

Alzheimer’s and dementia care require specialized techniques to ensure the safety and well-being of your loved one. Our caregivers are trained to manage memory loss, handle challenging behaviors, and offer emotional support, creating a secure and stimulating environment.

What if my loved one has a specific medical condition like ALS or MS?

We provide specialized care plans tailored to the needs of individuals with ALS, MS, or other medical conditions. Our caregivers are trained in managing symptoms, assisting with mobility, and offering the physical and emotional support your loved one needs.

Caregivers

How are caregivers selected?

We carefully match each caregiver to your loved one based on personality, preferences, and care needs. Our goal is to ensure compatibility, so your loved one receives the best possible care and companionship.

What qualifications do your caregivers have?

Our caregivers are fully trained and certified in their respective fields. They undergo extensive background checks and receive ongoing training to ensure they provide the highest standard of care.

Can I request a specific caregiver?

Yes, you can request a caregiver based on your preferences. We will do our best to match you with the caregiver who best fits your loved one’s needs and personality.

How do you handle emergencies?

Our caregivers are trained to handle emergencies calmly and efficiently. We also have a 24/7 support team available to assist in any situation.

Cost and insurance

Are your services covered by insurance?

Many of our services are covered by long-term care insurance, Medicaid, and other insurance providers. Contact us to discuss your insurance options and verify coverage.

What are the costs for home care services?

The cost depends on the type of care required and the level of support needed. Contact us to discuss your needs, and we will provide a personalized quote.

Care plans and scheduling

Can I change the care plan if needs change?

Yes! We offer flexible care plans that can be adjusted as your loved one’s needs evolve. We are committed to ensuring they receive the appropriate care at all times.

How do I stay updated on my loved one's care?

We utilize technology for easy communication, scheduling, and updates. You’ll be able to stay informed about your loved one’s care and progress through regular updates from our caregivers.

Can home care services be scheduled on short notice?

Yes! We offer flexible scheduling to accommodate urgent needs. Whether you need short-term care or ongoing support, we will work with you to find a solution quickly.

Will I be involved in the care planning process?

Absolutely! We believe in collaboration with families and keep you informed every step of the way. We work with you to create a customized care plan that best suits your loved one’s unique needs.

What happens if my loved one’s condition worsens?

Our caregivers are trained to handle changes in a client’s condition. We adjust care plans accordingly to ensure your loved one receives the appropriate level of care, no matter how their needs evolve.

Getting started

What is the process for getting started?

Getting started is simple. Reach out to us to schedule a consultation, and we’ll work with you to create a personalized care plan that meets your loved one’s needs. We're here to support you every step of the way.
